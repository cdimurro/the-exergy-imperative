# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, welcome evidence and correction, distinguish scientific
disagreement from personal criticism, protect confidential data, and explain
assumptions so contributors from different regions and industries can take part.
Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate misinformation, and
publication of others' private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer through the
contact address in `pyproject.toml`. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept
confidential to the extent possible. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
warn contributors, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban
participants when necessary.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
